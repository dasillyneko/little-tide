## ASCII Sharks

Source: https://github.com/stackedlol/ascii-sharks
Upstream commit: a0074c16a106f0abc665fe9712ca445f8f98ad8e

The rendering and schooling foundations in src/ocean/ are adapted from this MIT-licensed project. Little Tide adds original aquatic silhouettes, peaceful food attraction, population control, Twitch authorization/EventSub, server-owned interactions, persistence, streamer controls and an OBS view. These additions were built for the hackathon; the upstream engine is credited third-party work.
